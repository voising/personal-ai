What it is

Ollama, with zero configuration.

Ollama is the open-source engine most people use to run AI models on their own computer. It's powerful, but it expects you to use the Terminal and to know which model to pick. Personal AI is a small wrapper around it for everyone else. You open one app. It chooses the model, downloads it and keeps it running in the menu bar.

Personal AI doesThe setupDetects your Mac, picks a tested model, downloads it, starts at login.
Ollama doesThe engineRuns the model on your Mac's chip, bundled inside the app.
You doNothing elseOpen the app once. Any AI-enabled app on your Mac can now use it.

Already use Ollama? Personal AI finds it and uses it instead of starting a second copy.

Privacy

Everything stays on your Mac.

Personal AI only answers apps on your own computer. There's no account and no server in between. The model downloads once. After that, it works with Wi‑Fi turned off.

Out of your way

There when you ask. Gone when you don't.

The model loads when an app sends a request, in a few seconds. After 5 minutes without use it frees its memory. If macOS says memory is getting tight, it steps aside right away.
